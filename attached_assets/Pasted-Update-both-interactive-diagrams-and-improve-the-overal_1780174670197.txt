DIAGRAM 1
"Where IRS letters come from"
-----------------------------

MAIN MESSAGE

The IRS compares information from two streams:

A) Information reported by the taxpayer.

B) Information reported by employers, banks, brokers, payment processors, and other third parties.

Most IRS notices begin when those records do not match.
